Returns, Exchanges & Refunds

This page gives you all the details about Solace Apparel's policy and process for returns, exchanges, and refunds.

Policy Overview

Items can be returned or exchanged within 14 days of purchase with the original receipt.

Process

  • Bring the item to any Solace Apparel store.
  • Ensure the item is unworn, unwashed, and with original tags.
  • Show your receipt to our staff for processing.

Refunds

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method. Exchanges depend on stock availability.
